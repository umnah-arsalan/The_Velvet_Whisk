Welcome to The Velvet Whisk

For over sixty years, we have dedicated our craft to creating moments of absolute bliss for your palate, transforming premium ingredients into rich, unforgettable flavour experiences.

Every creation is designed to be a luxurious escape. From the very first bite, you are treated to a symphony of taste whether it’s the deep, melt-in-your-mouth richness of our signature dark chocolate ganache, the bright burst of fresh fruit compotes, or the velvety, cloud-like sweetness of our whipped creams.

When you treat yourself to a Velvet Whisk creation, you are stepping into a time-honoured tradition of luxury, comfort, and the absolute joy of a perfect flavour moment.

What We Offer

Cakes

From elegant celebrations to casual cravings, our cakes are layers of pure joy ultra-moist, perfectly sweet, and topped with luxurious buttercream.

Muffins

The ultimate breakfast companion. Bursting with fresh fruit, rich chocolate, or crunchy nuts, our muffins boast that perfect, golden domed top.

Brownies

An absolute dream for chocolate lovers. Intensely rich, deeply decadent, and complete with that coveted shiny, crinkly top crust.

Doughnuts

Pillowy, golden rings of perfection. Fried to a delicate crisp and finished with house-made glazes, rich fillings, or classic sprinkles.

Breads

Baked with patience and love. Crusty on the outside, pillowy-soft on the inside, our loaves are perfect for slicing, slathering in butter, or building the ultimate sandwich.

Cookies

Hand-scooped and baked to perfection. Expect a crispy golden edge with a soft, chewy heart that melts in your mouth.
